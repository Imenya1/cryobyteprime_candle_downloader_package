How to use

Fetch closed candle history from Deriv and export CSV files.

  1. Instruments — type symbols or use the grouped chips (Volatility, 1Hz, Boom/Crash, Forex).
  2. Date range — From / To (UTC), or Quick presets.
  3. Timeframes — pick under Minutes, Hours, or Daily.
  4. Press Download all. Each symbol × timeframe becomes a CSV.
  5. Download files individually or as a ZIP.

Log toggle hides the log and widens the results layout.

Serve over http://localhost. App ID 1089 works for public data.
